# Pragmatism

References: The Pragmatic Programmer, YAGNI, KISS, DRY.

## Guidelines

- Solve today's problem.
- Avoid speculative abstractions.
- Prefer simple solutions.
- Apply patterns only when they simplify the solution.
- Avoid unnecessary layers.
- Avoid unnecessary interfaces.
- Refactor when complexity appears.
- Favor incremental evolution.

## Never

- Do not design for hypothetical future requirements.
- Do not create abstractions with only one consumer unless architecturally justified.
- Do not introduce complexity without measurable benefit.
